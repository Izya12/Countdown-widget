Keep the dates that matter close at hand. Countdown is a local event tracker with round home-screen widgets, reminders and JSON backups.

• Create events with dates, optional times, notes and categories.
• Count down to future events or see the time since past dates.
• Repeat events daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, on weekdays or every N days.
• Add a round 1×1 widget with days or weeks, an event caption and an optional progress ring.
• Choose independent settings for each widget. Standard, minimal, progress and calendar widget styles are also available.
• Add reminder presets to saved events.
• Search, pin, favorite and archive events.
• Export and import local JSON backups.
• Use light, dark or system theme. English and Russian are supported.

No account, advertising, analytics or network backend. Events remain on your device unless you choose to export a backup.

Android may delay background widget updates and reminder delivery, especially in battery-saving modes. Notifications are approximate; this app is not an exact alarm clock. Widget size depends on your launcher. Open an event for a current countdown.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. Source code is available under the MIT license.
